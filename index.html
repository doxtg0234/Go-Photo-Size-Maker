Common passport photo sizes

Photo requirements are not identical worldwide. The United States commonly uses a 2 × 2 inch format, while many countries use metric formats such as 35 × 45 mm. Application rules can also differ by passport, visa, or document type, so always verify the final requirement with the official authority before submitting a photo.

How to prepare a passport photo online

  2. Upload the image using the photo maker above.
  3. Select the country and review the displayed dimensions.
  4. Download or print the result, then check the current official application instructions.

Frequently asked questions

Can I make a passport-size photo online?

Yes. The tool can resize and prepare an uploaded image in a selected photo format directly in the browser.

What size is a US passport photo?

The commonly used US passport photo format is 2 × 2 inches (51 × 51 mm). Confirm the current requirements with the US Department of State before applying.

Are passport photo requirements the same in every country?

No. Dimensions, background, facial position, and digital-file requirements can vary by country and application.
